# Hygeia ZhiVerse: review material

Images of a phone app and what it is measured against. The app is the companion to a home lingzhi grow pod (hardware plus a gamified app) shown at a booth. Offline; no server at the booth.

- `app-screens/`: the app as built, iPhone and iPad, rendered headless in Chrome (never yet seen on a real phone). `iphone-1-first` is the first screen. `iphone-5-play` is the fight.
- `art/`: spirits, story scenes, shop items.
- `references/`: Clash Royale and Honor of Kings store images, and a frame of the real pod.
- `MATRIX.md`: capabilities of five games against this app.
- `Golden rules.md`, `Table stakes.md`, `CONFIRMED.md`: how the app is meant to be built.
